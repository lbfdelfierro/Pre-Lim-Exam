      <h2 style="color: #2e4c23;">🤍 What I Created & The Concept</h2>
      <p style="color: #333;">
        For this activity, I created a promotional event poster for a free coffee drive called <b>"Coffee For All"</b> . 
      </p>
      <p style="color: #333;">
        <b>The Concept:</b> College life gets exhausting (especially during exam season!), so I wanted to design something that felt like a warm, energetic hug. Instead of a stiff, formal university announcement, I went for a fun, approachable vibe. Using cute little running coffee cups and casual text like "No Catch!" and "Just Caffeine & Good Vibes," the goal was to make students smile and actually want to show up to Arrupe Hall. It’s lighthearted, friendly, and totally matches that clean, positive aesthetic!
      </p>

      <h2 style="color: #2e4c23;">✨ Design Principles Applied</h2>
      <ul style="color: #333; line-height: 1.6;">
        <li><b>Visual Hierarchy:</b> I made sure your eyes know exactly where to go. <b>"COFFEE FOR ALL"</b> is the biggest text at the very top, immediately telling you what the poster is about. The secondary info (Date, Location) is slightly smaller but framed in bold banners, while the minor details are at the bottom.</li>
        <li><b>Contrast:</b> To make sure the poster is super readable (even from far away), I used a strong contrast between the dark, rich brown text and the soft cream background. No straining your eyes here!</li>
        <li><b>Balance & Symmetry:</b> The entire design is centered, giving it a very stable, clean look. To keep it from feeling boring, I added the two little running coffee cup characters on the left and right sides. They balance each other out perfectly so the poster doesn't feel heavy on one side.</li>
        <li><b>Simplicity (White Space):</b> I didn't clutter the poster with paragraphs of text. By leaving plenty of empty cream space around the graphics, the design feels airy, organized, and totally "clean girl" aesthetic. It gives the cute graphics room to breathe!</li>
      </ul>
