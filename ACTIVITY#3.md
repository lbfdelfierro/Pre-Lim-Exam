      <h2 style="color: #2e4c23;">🤍 What I Created & The Concept</h2>
      <p style="color: #333; line-height: 1.6;">
        For this activity, I conceptualized <b>SafeLine PH</b>, an anonymous cyberbullying reporting system designed specifically for Filipino students[cite: 1]. The output includes a detailed project proposal (<code>Project Documentation_DELFIERRO.pdf</code>) and an engaging social media infographic to promote it. 
      </p>
      <p style="color: #333; line-height: 1.6;">
        <b>The Concept:</b> Cyberbullying is a growing concern, but Filipino students often hesitate to report these incidents due to <i>hiya</i> (embarrassment), fear of retaliation, or uncertainty about whether adults will actually help[cite: 1]. SafeLine PH bridges this gap by providing a safe, anonymous first step for students to ask for help without the pressure of an immediate face-to-face confrontation[cite: 1].
      </p>

      <h2 style="color: #2e4c23;">🌿 How I Developed the Output</h2>
      <p style="color: #333; line-height: 1.6;">
        I based the core framework of SafeLine PH on the "Say Something Anonymous Reporting System" developed by Sandy Hook Promise, modifying it to fit our local school settings[cite: 1]. I made sure to emphasize that this system is human-reviewed by trained personnel, like guidance counselors, rather than relying on AI[cite: 1]. This ensures that every report is met with genuine human empathy and appropriate action[cite: 1].
      </p>
      <p style="color: #333; line-height: 1.6;">
        For the documentation, I grounded the proposal in local research from the Davao Region to prove the immediate need for this system[cite: 1]. For the infographic, I summarized this heavy academic research into bite-sized, digestible sections (The Problem, The Barrier, The Solution) so it would perform well on social media and immediately grab a student's attention.
      </p>

      <h2 style="color: #2e4c23;">✨ Design Principles Applied </h2>
      <ul style="color: #333; line-height: 1.6;">
        <li><b>Proximity & Grouping:</b> Instead of throwing a wall of text at the viewer, I grouped related information into distinct, semi-transparent panels. By keeping "The Problem" physically separated from the "How To" section, it creates "bite-sized" chunks of info that are way less overwhelming for a student to process.</li>
        <li><b>Visual Hierarchy:</b> I designed the flow so your eyes know exactly the order to read things. The massive, glowing "SAFELINE PH" title grabs your attention first. Then, the bold subheadings guide you down the page step-by-step, leading you naturally to the most actionable part: the scan-ready QR code.</li>
        <li><b>High Contrast:</b> Since the background uses a dark, moody blue-and-purple gradient, I used crisp white and bright neon colors for the typography. This sharp contrast ensures the text is incredibly readable on mobile screens while still giving off that modern, digital "cyber" aesthetic.</li>
        <li><b>Alignment & Balance:</b> The main structural elements (like the titles, the panels, and the character at the bottom) are strongly center-aligned. This creates a stable, balanced foundation so the graphic doesn't feel top-heavy. However, inside the panels, I used left-alignment for the longer text to keep it clean and easy to read!</li>
      </ul>
