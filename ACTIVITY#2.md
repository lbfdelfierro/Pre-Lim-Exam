    <td bgcolor="#CCFDA9" style="padding: 25px;">
      <h2 style="color: #2e4c23;">🤍 What I Created & The Concept</h2>
      <p style="color: #333; line-height: 1.6;">
        For this activity, I developed the visual brand identity for <b>Louie's Cinnamon Rolls</b>. This includes a primary logo, a contact/business card layout, and a comprehensive style guide breaking down the colors and fonts. 
      </p>
      <p style="color: #333; line-height: 1.6;">
        <b>The Concept:</b> The goal was to build a brand that feels incredibly warm, homemade, and approachable. Blending the busy life of a "Student Nurse" with a "Home Baker" meant avoiding anything too stiff, clinical, or overly corporate. I wanted the design to feel like a cozy, sweet treat from a friend—authentic, comforting, and full of personality!
      </p>
 </tr>
  <tr>
    <td bgcolor="#E4ECE0" style="padding: 25px;">
      <h2 style="color: #2e4c23;">🌿 How I Developed the Output</h2>
      <p style="color: #333; line-height: 1.6;">
        I started by zeroing in on the literal product to set the mood. Cinnamon rolls naturally evoke feelings of comfort, so I wanted the visuals to match that energy. I designed the main logo first, having a little fun by replacing the "O" in "ROLLS" with a swirly cinnamon roll graphic to make it instantly recognizable.
      </p>
      <p style="color: #333; line-height: 1.6;">
        From there, I expanded the branding by adding a rustic "Freshly Baked" banner for secondary logo variations. Finally, I tied it all together into a clean, organized contact card layout. I kept the background crisp white so the warm colors could pop, ensuring all the essential contact info is highlighted without overwhelming the viewer.
      </p>
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td bgcolor="#CCFDA9" style="padding: 25px; border-radius: 0 0 15px 15px;">
      <h2 style="color: #2e4c23;">✨ Why I Selected These Colors & Typography</h2>
      <ul style="color: #333; line-height: 1.6;">
        <li><b>The Color Palette:</b> I pulled these shades straight from the oven! The palette features <b>Dark Red (#531f17)</b>, <b>Gray Orange (#ba5c28)</b>, <b>Brown (#a2446)</b>, and <b>Orange (#e79f6f)</b>. These colors directly mimic the baking process—from the dark, rich cinnamon spice to the warm, golden-brown dough. It creates an appetizing, cozy vibe that instantly communicates what the brand is selling.</li>
        <li><b>The Typography:</b> I selected two playful, handwritten-style fonts: <b>Marykate</b> and <b>Sweet & Salty</b>. Standard corporate fonts felt way too boring and rigid for a bakery. These specific fonts look genuinely handcrafted, which perfectly matches the home baker energy. They are super friendly and visually interesting, but still clean enough to be easily readable on a small business card!</li>
